Openers

ranked by reply rate

Mel leads with the top one. The weak openers stay listed so you can see what it has moved away from.

1 “I pulled what your place would go for this spring — want the number?” Mel leads with this 25%
2 “A neighbour just closed at $1.2M. Want me to run yours?” 18%
3 “Quick one — are you still looking in Redlands, or has that changed?” 12%
4 “Checking in to see how things are going with the house.” Not landing 7%
5 “Hi! I’m reaching out about your property. Let me know if you’d like to chat.” Not landing 6%
Mel tries it on the next batch and reports the reply rate here.

Questions

ranked by reply rate

What Mel has asked in past outreach, and what it got back.

1 “What’s your timeline — this spring, or later in the year?” Mel leads with this 31%
2 “Do you need to buy next, or is this a straight sale?” 22%
3 “What would the number have to be for you to move?” 14%
4 “Have you had a valuation done recently?” Not landing 9%
5 “Is there anything holding you back right now?” Not landing 5%
Mel works it into the next conversations and reports back.
